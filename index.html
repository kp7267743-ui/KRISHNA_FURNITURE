Krishna Kitchen Trolley and Modular Furniture was born from a single conviction: your home deserves furniture that truly fits — not the closest standard size, not a compromise, but the exact right thing for your exact space.

Vinod Prajapati has spent over a decade crafting kitchens, wardrobes, TV units, and bedroom furniture across Nashik. Every project begins with a personal site visit and ends with an installation you can feel confident about.
